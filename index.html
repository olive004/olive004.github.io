Hi, I'm Olivia.

I'm a machine learning researcher and engineer developing generative models and inference-time steering for molecular design, in JAX and PyTorch. My background is in synthetic biology, RNA circuit design and bioprocess modelling.

About

I'm a Research Engineer Intern at InstaDeep in London, where I work on inference-time reward steering for Boltz protein cofolding, including steering with machine-learned interatomic potentials (MLIPs). I also co-developed ALF, an open-source active learning framework for scientific discovery across protein, molecule and materials design.

I did my DPhil in Engineering Science at the University of Oxford in the Harrison Steel Lab (Control Group), supported by the Wadham College John Brookman Scholarship. There I built a vectorised JAX simulation framework and inverse sequence design tools for synthetic RNA circuits, and trained conditional VAEs on accelerated dynamical simulations to design circuits for both function and evolutionary robustness.

During my DPhil, I modelled AAV production at Asimov in Boston, combining Bayesian optimisation, mechanistic models and ML to prioritise process-development experiments. Before that, I studied Molecular Bioengineering (MEng) at Imperial College London.

I care about open science, reproducible research and building tools that other researchers actually use.

Research interests

  • Generative models for molecular design Diffusion and flow models and VAEs for proteins, small molecules and biological sequences.
  • Foundation models for biology Protein and DNA language models for predicting protein function and gene expression from sequence.
  • Inference-time steering Reward tilting and guidance of pretrained generative models, such as cofolding models, towards physical and functional objectives.
  • Active learning & Bayesian optimisation Choosing the next experiment when every label costs a wet-lab assay or a long simulation.
  • Differentiable simulation for synthetic biology JAX-accelerated dynamical models and inverse design of RNA genetic circuits.
